Research date: 2026-09-07 (IST). Scope: existing main-site chapters, repository inspection and public-source research only. No chapter rewritten; no publishing, paid access, local interview, or first-hand visit performed.

## Topic and priority decision

**Retain Kamrunag as the first deeply researched chapter, not as the strongest measured ranking opportunity.** The existing agent system explicitly prioritizes Kamrunag, and the two existing chapter slugs are `kamrunag-the-lake-of-oaths` and `saroa-to-kamrunag`.[16][14][15] Historical GSC summaries instead show `mural danda trek` as the clearer measured destination signal and `/temples` as a broader topical opportunity; no Kamrunag-specific performance row was found in those summaries.[11][13] Thus Kamrunag is an editorial/local-trust priority, not a demonstrated high-volume keyword winner. See `historical-gsc.md` for dated metrics and limitations.

The research angle is the relationship between a sacred lake, reported offerings, changing access and the surrounding forest—not a treasure story. This is **EDITORIAL INTERPRETATION**, not a claim about what every devotee believes.

## Evidence classification

- **VERIFIED FACT**: a bounded geographical observation, documented event, or explicitly attributed research finding; not proof of supernatural efficacy. Local gate `verified` is restricted to an official source or named source appropriate to the claim.
- **LOCAL TRADITION**: a belief or practice documented by a source; publish as that source's account of tradition, never as historical or supernatural fact.
- **ORAL HISTORY**: a named narrator's recorded account with date, consent and context. No original oral-history interview was obtained in this audit. Newspaper interviews are mediated reporting, not our fieldwork.
- **EDITORIAL INTERPRETATION**: the writer's metaphor, inference or recommendation, visibly separated from local knowledge.
- **UNVERIFIED CLAIM**: insufficient evidence; omit, qualify, or obtain the exact missing verification. `unsafe` claims must not be dressed up with vague “locals say.”

These distinctions implement the repository's blocking local-verification gate and its preference for omission over an invented cultural claim.[17]

## Conflicts and claims requiring a hold

1. **Altitude and route distances:** official 3,334 m / approximately 6 km from Rohanda contrasts with botanist GPS approximately 2,900 m / 5.5 km; TOI's 2015 report says 8 km and HT's 2014 report says 5 km.[1][9][6] The HT route wording is separately preserved in its evidence file.[8] These cannot be silently harmonized: endpoint, route variant and measurement method are unresolved. Retain provenance, prefer official approximate baseline, seek a dated local trace.
2. **“Nothing is ever taken out” / “never robbed”:** current chapter uses absolutes and a treasury framing.[14] A report of planned lake cleaning exists; it does not prove valuables were removed, but shows management and intervention cannot be replaced with a universal non-interference claim.[8] **UNVERIFIED CLAIM**, `unsafe` as an absolute. Do not turn devotees' lack of knowledge of theft into a comprehensive theft record.[6]
3. **“Lake of oaths,” promises sealed with coins, “contract law older than paper”:** the chapter presents these as local truth, but official evidence describes offerings on fulfilment of wishes, not a documented oath tribunal or customary legal process.[14][1] Treat the title/metaphor as **EDITORIAL INTERPRETATION**; cultural/legal assertions are **UNVERIFIED CLAIM**, `needs-local-source`. No chapter-title or slug change is proposed here.
4. **First-person episode and quote:** the chapter's family, grandmother, shopkeeper and “Woh unka hai” scene has no author/date/recording provenance in the inspected file.[14] This is **UNVERIFIED CLAIM**, not an interview this audit verified. Ask the founder for original field notes, real speaker context and quotation consent; absence of documentation is not proof the encounter never happened.
5. **Kamrunag / Barbarika / Khatu Shyam equivalence:** detailed versions circulate in the botanist's article, but its cultural story is unsourced and internally shifts between body/head descriptions.[9] The repository already flags this equivalence as requiring a Mandi source.[17] **LOCAL TRADITION variant / UNVERIFIED CLAIM of equivalence**, `needs-local-source`; do not merge variants into one authoritative history.
6. **Women/caste access:** the only named-official access statement retrieved is a temporary 2014 cleaning restriction reported by HT/IANS.[8] No permanent restriction or current eligibility policy is verified. Do not universalize it or use it as present travel advice.
7. **Season/fitness assurance:** “any reasonably fit walker” and fixed easy/moderate grading are chapter assertions, not route-specific risk assessments.[14][15] **UNVERIFIED CLAIM**, `needs-local-source`; refuse safety guarantees.

**ORAL HISTORY status: none collected.** Proposed questions, not contact attempts or invented testimony:

- To a currently serving, named temple committee representative or priest: What words do devotees use for offerings? Are vows a distinct practice? What may a respectful visitor observe or photograph? What must visitors not put in the lake?
- To a named elder/knowledge holder: Which Kamrunag origin narrative do you tell, in which language and village context? Is Yaksha/Barbarika/Khatu Shyam an accepted identity here or one variant? Record disagreements without adjudicating belief.
- To committee/local administration: What are this year's fair dates, current access rules and cleaning arrangements? May the statement be published with your name, role and confirmation date?
- To local road/forest authority and a named route walker: Where are today's drivable endpoint and safe walking approaches from Rohanda, Shala and Saroa? Which route coordinates, closures and distances can be evidenced?
- To the Biodiversity Board: Provide final notification number/date/boundary if designation occurred, or confirm proposal status. To researchers: request a freely shareable methods/full-text version, without purchasing access.
