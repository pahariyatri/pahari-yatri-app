# Kamrunag lake where offerings are deposited for centuries

Located at an altitude of 3334 meters above sea level, Kamrunag lake of Mandi district was visited by thousands of people on Monday who had tracked around 8 km steep incline to witness the special ritual of immersing currency notes, gold and silver jewelry worth lakhs in the lakh.

![Kamrunag lake where offerings are deposited for centuries](https://static.toiimg.com/thumb/msid-47529300,imgsize-110164,width-400,resizemode-4/47529300.jpg)

SHIMLA: Located at an altitude of 3334 meters above sea level, Kamrunag lake of Mandi district was visited by thousands of people on Monday who had tracked around 8 km steep incline to witness the special ritual of immersing currency notes, gold and silver jewelry worth lakhs in the lakh. In Kamrunag temple money and jewellery offered by devotees is not deposited in the banks rarher immersed in the lake water.This trend is being followed by centuries.
 Each year, thousands of devotees throng Kamrunag temple from across the state to witness three days fair after completing arduous journey on foot. On the fulfillment of their wish they make offerings to the Kamrunag deity which later is immersed in the lake.
 "No one knows how much currency notes, coins, gold and silver jewellery is settled in the base of lake as due to religious significance people does not step inside the lake. But it is a common site to see currency notes floating on the lake water," said Gian Thakur from Shimla who was among the devotees visiting the temple on Monday.
 Kamrunag is considered as weather god and temple is located in the Seraj valley of Mandi district earlier there was tradition of animal sacrifice during the three days fair but this time no sacrifice took place as Himachal Pradesh High Court has imposed a ban on animal sacrifice.
 "Kamrunag lake is a live example of strange religious traditions followed in Himachal Pradesh. For ages currency notes, coins and ornaments are being immersed in the lake water and no one dares to dig them out fearing the wrath of Kamrunag deity. We never have heard of any theft incident at this lake which houses valuables in crores," said Neelam Thakur.
 To reach Kamrunag one has to first reach Rohanda located some 60 km from Mandi town from where the arduous trek of 8 km starts. "It is believed that on offering gold, silver, currency notes or coins, deity fulfills the wish of devotees. It is not uncommon to see devotees making offerings in the lake," she said.
 Garima Singh another devotee said that for last three days thousand of devotees had made offerings at the Kamrunag temple and on Monday around 2.30 a special ritual was followed which perhaps not followed anywhere else in the world.
 "Temple priest and others collected all the offerings of all three days and immersed it in the lake water. This shows how much deep religious faith people have in the lake. It is not the temples alone which are having immense wealth even this lake is no less as till date no one has dared to check its depth," said Kapil another devotee.
