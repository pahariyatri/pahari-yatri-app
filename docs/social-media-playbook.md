# Pahari Yatri — Social Media Playbook

How to run @pahariyatri so every post *feels* like the library: calm, cinematic, human, and worth saving. Built from the HOOK / PSYCH / PERSONA / STRAT decks, wired to the content that now exists on the site.

---

## 1. The Feel (non-negotiables)

**One sentence:** a Himalayan editorial journal that teaches, told by people you believe.

**Every post must pass three checks:**
1. *Would a local nod at this?* (accuracy + respect)
2. *Would a stressed person in a metro save this?* (recognition + calm)
3. *Does it teach one thing?* (a belief, a rule, a place, a word)

**Visual feel:** natural light only, slow movement, real sound (river/bells/wind — no trending audio by default), muted palette matching the site (pine green, charcoal, warm off-white). Text on screen: sparse, serif for quotes, 1–2 lines max.

**Voice feel:** low intensity, always (per the Psych Matrix — the brand persuades by recognition, never pressure). Banned: "don't miss," "must visit," "hidden gem," exclamation marks, countdown urgency, package words (itinerary, deal, booking).

## 2. The System: WHEN-first content

The persona deck's core idea: people act in **WHEN moments**, not demographics. Every reel targets a *moment*, not an audience:

| WHEN moment (viewer's life) | Story/chapter to point at |
|---|---|
| Just lost a job / startup died | Kasol Slow Saturday |
| New parent, no silence in years | River Hymn |
| Health scare in the family | Kheerganga Fire & Water |
| Just retired / big identity shift | Bhaba Moon Road |
| Someone leaving (visa, marriage, move) | Rupin Waterfall Days |
| Burnout, over-measured life | Churdhar Bell Echo, Quiet Compass |
| Recovery (illness, surgery, grief) | Meadow Reverie, Jalori Small Circle, Small God |
| "What am I doing with my life" (young) | Solstice Fire |
| Fear that's been deciding things | Buran First Light |

**Rule:** the first line of the reel names the WHEN, not the place. The place is the answer, so it comes second.

## 3. The Franchises (episodic > one-offs)

The STRAT deck is clear: series beat singles because open loops bring people back. Run these four shows, each with a fixed repeatable format and ONE variable per episode:

### Show 1 — "Why I Walked" (flagship, 1×/week)
- Format: 30–45s. Opens in the narrator's *world* (a desk, a hospital corridor, a school blackboard, a wedding shoot) → hard cut to the trail. Voiceover reads the story's opening lines. Ends: "Read the full story in the Library — link in bio."
- Variable per episode: the WHEN moment. Fixed: structure, serif end-card, closing formula.
- Season 1 = the 17 story openings, already written (see `content-psychology-map.md`). That's 17 episodes banked.
- **Andromeda note (HOOK deck):** Meta groups ads/posts that *look* similar in the first 3 seconds. Because each episode opens in a different world (bank, school, gym, studio), every episode reads as fresh creative. This is the whole trick — never open two episodes on the same visual.

### Show 2 — "One Rule of the Mountains" (1×/week)
- Format: 7–15s. One Yatri Code principle or etiquette truth per episode, one static shot, one line of serif text. ("Take nothing from Kamrunag's water. It is his." / "Ask before you photograph a face or a god.")
- Highly shareable/savable — this is the saves engine.

### Show 3 — "The Story of This Place in 30 Seconds" (1×/week)
- Format: chapter teasers. B-roll + the chapter's hook line ("Every roof they built for her fell down. So they stopped building roofs."). End-card: "Chapter 4 · The Winter Book · link in bio."
- Numbering episodes ("Chapter 4 of 25") creates the collection instinct.

### Show 4 — "Questions Yatris Ask" (stories/carousel, 1×/week)
- Format: carousel or story-poll from the chapters' FAQ banks (already 3–4 per chapter on the site). Slide 1 = the question. Answer teaches; last slide links the chapter.

## 4. Hook bank (openings, adapted per the 7 types)

Use these text/voice hooks; pair each with a *visually distinct* first frame:

- **Permission Slip:** "You are not a bad mother for wanting three days of silence."
- **Villain Swap:** "The reason you can't enjoy mountains isn't your fitness. It's your watch."
- **Bold Claim:** "There's a lake in Himachal holding centuries of gold. Nobody steals it."
- **Curiosity Gap:** "Every roof they built for this goddess fell down. So they stopped."
- **Before/After:** "Before Manali winter: everyone asking what I'm doing with my life. After: I can split wood in four strokes."
- **Problem Statement:** "If your last three trips were just photo lists — this library was written for you."

**4-layer check per reel:** unexpected first frame (Stop) → WHEN line (Relate) → the belief shift (Believe) → the trail/payoff + CTA (Continue). If a reel stops at Relate, it's not done.

---

## 5. Psychological coverage (monthly review)

Once a month, tag the month's posts against the matrix (valence × self-anchor, like the site's story table). Rules of thumb:
- Never two consecutive posts from the same cell.
- High Negative is allowed only if the same post resolves it (this brand never *ends* in fear).
- If everything trends aspirational/ideal-self, ship two Actual-Self recognition posts next week.

- **Week 1 — The Door:** manifesto reel ("Experience the Himalayas like a Yatri, not a tourist" over slow b-roll) · Yatri Code carousel (8 slides) · 1 "One Rule" short.
- **Week 2 — The Library Opens:** the four seasonal books introduced as book covers (carousel) · first "Why I Walked" (Kasol — the startup one; broadest WHEN) · 1 chapter teaser (Shikari Devi — strongest curiosity gap).
- **Week 3 — The Voices:** 2 more "Why I Walked" (River Hymn, Buran First Light — different worlds, different cells) · first FAQ carousel (Kamrunag: "Can you take a coin from the lake?").
- **Week 4 — The Loop:** folklore post (the aabi bird of Serolsar) · "One Rule" · first contribute call: "Your WHEN moment + your trail. The library has a shelf waiting." → `/contribute`.

**Metrics that matter:** saves & shares > likes; link-in-bio taps to /library; contribute submissions; WhatsApp joins. Ignore follower count for 90 days.

## 9. Production reality (phone-only is fine)

- Shoot vertical 4K on a phone, lock exposure, never digital-zoom.
- Golden hours only for landscape; indoor "world" openings can be any light — real beats pretty for the Stop layer.
- Subtitles always (most viewing is muted); serif for quotes, sans for speech.
- Batch: one trail day = 1 chapter teaser + 2 "One Rule" + b-roll for 2 "Why I Walked" episodes.
- Voiceovers: record in a quiet room reading the story's actual opening paragraphs. The writing is already done — that's the point of the library.
