# Lahaul and Spiti: The High Desert

Crossing the Rohtang or the Atal Tunnel brings you into a world of transformation. Spiti is 'The Middle Land' between India and Tibet.

## Entity Insight
The Key Gompa in Spiti and the Mrikula Devi temple in Udaipur (Lahaul) are two of the most significant architectural entities in the Himalayas. They are not just buildings; they are repositories of ancient cosmic maps.
