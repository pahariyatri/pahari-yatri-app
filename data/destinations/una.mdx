# Una: The Spiritual Foothills

Una is the first touch of the Himalayas for those arriving from the Punjab plains. It is a land of red soil and intense faith.

## Local Knowledge
The Baba Rudranand Ashram is a site of eternal fire (dhuna) that has been burning for centuries. It's the most authentic spot for a quiet, ego-dissolving visit.
