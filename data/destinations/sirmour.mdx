# Sirmour: The Sacred South

From the fossil parks of Suketi to the peaks of Churdhar, Sirmour offers a bridge between the plains and the high Himalayas.

## Local Reality
Renuka Lake is not just a picnic spot; it is a sacred geological anomaly shaped like a sleeping goddess. Respect the silence here.
