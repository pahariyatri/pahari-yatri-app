# Kangra: The Fortress Valley

Kangra is the meeting point of the plains and the Dhauladhar. It is a land of intense history and spiritual power.

## Entity Insight
The Masroor Rock Cut Temples in Kangra are often called the 'Ellora of the North'. They represent a monolithic achievement that most Himalayan guidebooks ignore.
