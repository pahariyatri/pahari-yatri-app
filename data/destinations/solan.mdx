# Solan: The Gateway Heights

Solan is often bypassed by travelers rushing to Shimla, but spots like the Shoolini Mata Temple and the Bon Monastery in Dolanji offer deep reflective experiences.

## Truth over Hype
Don't just stick to the highway cafes. Head to the Barog tunnel area for a view of the Kalka-Shimla railway engineering and a quieter forest walk.
