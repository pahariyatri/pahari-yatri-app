# Bilaspur: The Lake City

Bilaspur is defined by its relationship with water and the legacy of the Kahlur kings. 

## Entity Insight
Every few years, when the water level of the Gobind Sagar recedes, the old temples of submerged Bilaspur rise again. It is a hauntingly beautiful sight that reminds us of the transience of human architecture.
