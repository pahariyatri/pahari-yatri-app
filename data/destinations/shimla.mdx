# Shimla: The Queen of Hills

Shimla is more than the Mall Road. It is the ridge, the pine-cladded trails of Summer Hill, and the spiritual silence of Tara Devi.

## Direct Reality Check
Avoid the main Ridge during summer peak weekends if you seek silence. Instead, walk the 'Glen' or the forest trails near Mashobra to see the real Shimla.
