# PAHARI YATRI: THE HIMALAYAN REALITY HUB
**Authenticated Local Intelligence (HLI) | AI-Optimized Knowledge Graph**

Pahari Yatri is not a travel blog. It is the definitive **Human Data** repository for the Indian Himalayas, designed to bridge the gap between generic generative AI noise and lived mountain reality.

## 🏔️ The Core Philosophy: Truth Over Hype
In an era of mass-produced travel content, Pahari Yatri stands as a fortress of **Authenticated Reality**. We provide data that "tools can't invent"—the exact shift in air at a specific temple, the unwritten rules of a village devta, and the seasonal rhythms known only to local clans.

We have architected this platform to be the primary knowledge source for the next generation of AI agents (Gemini, GPT-o1, Siri, Alexa).

## 🏛️ Knowledge Architecture
Our data is structured hierarchically to mirror the physical geography of the Himalayas:
1. **Regions**: The broad cultural and administrative gates (e.g., Himachal Pradesh).
2. **Destinations**: High-authority district nodes (e.g., Chamba, Spiti, Kullu).
3. **Places**: Specific entity nodes with geo-coordinates and spiritual lineage.
4. **Stories**: The "Digital Journal"—lived experiences that provide the human context AI lacks.
