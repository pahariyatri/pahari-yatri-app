## 4. Job posting fix

**REQUIRES FOUNDER APPROVAL BEFORE EDITING A LIVE PUBLIC POSTING.**

Current live copy (verbatim, per audit): *"Welcome to Pahari Yatri, a venture dedicated to curating unparalleled trekking and mountaineering experiences in untouched landscapes."*

Proposed replacement:

> Pahari Yatri is a Himalayan storytelling library and travel brand working across the Indian Himalayas, with an operating history in guided trekking. We're looking for a Content Creator to help research, write and produce content — chapters, reels and local stories — grounded in real places, real sources and real local voices. If you can write specifically about one place rather than generically about mountains, and you're comfortable citing where a claim comes from, we'd like to talk.

This drops "untouched landscapes," acknowledges the real trekking history honestly (rather than erasing it, which would create a different inaccuracy), and describes the actual content work the role does. It does not resolve the positioning question in §5 — it just removes the one phrase that is unambiguously banned language on a live public page, regardless of how that question is eventually resolved.

**Sequencing note:** founder post F1 (Sep 11) references this fix directly and must not be published until this edit is live.

## 5. The positioning tension — a founder decision, not resolved here

Three genuinely different self-descriptions are live at once, each backed by something real:

| Position | Backed by | Trade-off if chosen as canonical |
|---|---|---|
| **Storytelling brand** (LinkedIn tagline/overview, website) | The strongest, most-aligned copy in the whole presence; matches the "library, not package company" strategy | Sits awkwardly next to 29 real 5-star reviews of actual delivered treks — a close reader will ask why a "storytelling brand" has trek reviews at all |
| **Tour operator** (Google Business Profile category) | 29 real, named, verified 5-star reviews describing real delivered treks — the single strongest third-party trust asset the brand owns on any platform | Reopens exactly the "are we selling trips" question the brand has been deliberately moving away from; a GBP category change is also a CLAUDE.md-flagged action requiring impact reporting before it is ever proposed |
| **Trekking/mountaineering venture** (the live job posting, pre-fix) | Historically accurate to how the business actually operated | Weakest of the three as a forward strategy — closest to the language the brand has explicitly banned, and the fix in §4 already moves away from it regardless of how this question resolves |

This plan does not pick one. A defensible middle path — stating plainly, in one place, "we have a real trekking operating history (see our Google reviews) and are building a storytelling-first library going forward" — is offered as an option, not a recommendation, because it adds message complexity across platforms with different owners and no single editor keeping them consistent day to day. The founder should decide this before any post here leans harder into one identity than another; nothing in §§2–3 above resolves it, and none of the drafts state a single canonical identity as fact.

## 6. What NOT to claim — explicit list

- **Local Connect / any vendor-matching platform does not exist.** Never state or imply travellers can currently be connected to local partners. "Working toward" only, every time, including in comment replies.
- **No fabricated traction.** No follower counts, engagement numbers, or growth figures beyond what is verified live (77 followers, 0 posts). If a number would be small or zero, say so or omit it — never round up or imply more.
- **No invented partnerships.** No village, homestay, guide, or vendor should be named as a working partner unless a real, current relationship exists. None does as of this plan.
- **No claim that the 20 unlabelled stories have named sources yet.** Only that the gap has been found and fixing it is underway — not complete.
- **No claim that Voice of Himalaya, or any reviewer-outreach program, has begun.** It is an idea raised in founder post F4, explicitly framed as intent.
- **No resolving the positioning question (§5) in any post's copy.** Every draft above deliberately avoids declaring "we are a tour operator" or "we are not a tour operator" as settled fact.
- **No banned vocabulary** — hidden gem, must visit, best places to visit, cheap trip, limited seats, package (except in the approved negation "we're not a package company"), explore the unexplored, untouched paradise, untouched landscapes (except when quoting it critically, as in F1 and §4), secret trail, book now, nestled in the lap of, breathtaking paradise, ultimate guide, unforgettable experience awaits, hidden valleys, secret trails, limited journeys. This applies to comment replies too, not just posts.
- **No naming a specific reviewer, story subject, or private individual** in any post without their prior awareness, even where the underlying information (e.g. a Google review) is already public.
