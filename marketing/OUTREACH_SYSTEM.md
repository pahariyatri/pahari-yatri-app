**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Owner:** `outreach-relationship-agent`
**Status:** drafts only. **No message here has been sent, and no agent can send one.**

## 1. The rule that governs everything below

**Agents draft. The founder personalizes. The founder sends. Personally, from their own account.**

This is not a formality. Pahari Yatri's entire premise is local truth over scenery. A copy-pasted DM blast contradicts that premise more completely than a bad Reel ever could, because the recipients are exactly the people the brand wants to work with later — creators, homestay owners, guides, photographers. They receive template outreach constantly. They can tell.

Per this repo's approval policy, sending a message to a real person is outside agent authority. There is no automation step in this system and none should be added.

### The workflow

```
1. IDENTIFY   → founder or agent finds a real, specific person
2. RESEARCH   → founder answers the personalization questions (agent lists them)
3. DRAFT      → agent drafts ONE message for ONE person
4. REVIEW     → founder edits in their own voice
5. SEND       → founder sends, manually, from their own account
6. LOG        → founder records: date, person, channel, response
7. FOLLOW UP  → at most once, only with a new reason. Then stop.
```

Step 2 is the one that gets skipped, and skipping it is what turns this into spam.

## 2. The personalization standard

**A variable is not a blank. It is a research requirement.**

| Bad | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| `Hi {NAME}, love your {CONTENT}!` | Fills in from anything. Reads as a mail merge because it is one. |
| `Your {PLACE} content is amazing` | Compliment inflation with no evidence of having watched anything. |

| Good | Why it works |
|---|---|
| `{SPECIFIC_POST}` — *must be one post you actually watched, named by its subject, not its metrics* | Cannot be filled without doing the work. |
| `{WHY_THEM}` — *the specific reason this person and not a similar one* | If you cannot answer it, do not send the message. |

**If a draft below could be sent to fifty people without changes, it is broken.** Report it rather than sending it.

### Template A — Instagram DM, micro creator collaboration

```
CHANNEL         — Instagram DM
RECIPIENT TYPE  — micro creator / Himachal photographer
GOAL            — one genuine conversation. Not a deal.
RESEARCH NEEDED — 1. One specific post of theirs, watched fully. What was it about?
                  2. Do they live in Himachal or visit it? Affects the whole message.
                  3. Have they posted about overtourism, local culture, or village
                     rules? If yes, lead with that.
                  4. Is their content place-led or self-led? Only place-led is a fit.

VARIABLES       — {SPECIFIC_POST}  the actual subject of one post you watched.
                                   "your Tirthan post" is valid.
                                   "your amazing content" is not.
                  {WHAT_YOU_NOTICED} the specific choice they made — what they
                                   left out, how long they held a shot, what they
                                   refused to name. Proves you watched.
                  {THEIR_PLACE}    a place they clearly know well.

DRAFT           —
Hi {NAME} — your {SPECIFIC_POST} came up and I ended up watching it twice.
{WHAT_YOU_NOTICED} is not a common choice in Himachal content.

I run Pahari Yatri. It is a Himalayan story library, not an agency — chapters
about places, and stories from people who actually know them. Nothing to sell.

I am starting a series called Voice of Himalaya: one real person, one real place,
one thing they know that a visitor would not. You would be a good fit for
{THEIR_PLACE}.

It would be a conversation, your name on your own words, and you would see it
before anything goes up. No exclusivity, no deliverables.

Genuinely no problem if this is not your thing.

WHY THIS WORKS  — Names a specific post, admits there is nothing to sell, makes
                  a small ask, gives an easy exit.
IF NO REPLY     — Do not follow up. A second DM to a creator who ignored the
                  first is where this becomes spam.
RISK            — Do not state or imply a follower count, theirs or ours.
                  Do not promise reach or payment.
```

### Template B — LinkedIn connection note, tourism professional

LinkedIn connection notes cap near 300 characters. Written to that limit.

```
CHANNEL         — LinkedIn connection request
RECIPIENT TYPE  — tourism professional / adventure company / homestay operator
GOAL            — connection accepted. Nothing more at this stage.
RESEARCH NEEDED — 1. Their actual role and region.
                  2. Something they wrote or built. Needed for {THEIR_WORK}.
                  3. Are they a potential future Local Connect partner? Changes
                     nothing in this message — it must NOT hint at that.

VARIABLES       — {THEIR_WORK}  a specific thing they have done or written.
                  {REGION}      their actual working region.

DRAFT           —
Hi {NAME} — I write Pahari Yatri, a Himalayan story library covering {REGION}.
{THEIR_WORK} was useful to me. Not pitching anything, I would just like to
follow people doing real work in the region.

WHY THIS WORKS  — Under 300 characters, states the non-pitch plainly, asks for
                  nothing.
IF NO REPLY     — None. A connection request is not followed up.
RISK            — Never mention Local Connect, vendors, onboarding, or
                  partnership at connection stage. That is the message that gets
                  the founder marked as a recruiter.
```

## 4. Story submission requests — the Voice of Himalaya supply chain

**This is the most important section here.** Per the audit, the series cannot be sourced from the existing archive — not one of the 28 stories carries a named human source. Every Voice of Himalaya entry starts with one of these messages.

### Template C — local voice request

```
CHANNEL         — LinkedIn, Instagram, or in person (in person is better)
RECIPIENT TYPE  — elder, homestay host, guide, temple committee member, shopkeeper
GOAL            — one real, consented, named story
RESEARCH NEEDED — 1. Their name, correctly spelled, and their village.
                  2. Their actual relationship to the place. Born there? Working
                     there? This determines what they can speak to.
                  3. The specific thing you want to ask about — one thing.
                  4. Which chapter it would sit under.
                  5. Their language preference. Draft in Hindi/Pahari if that is
                     what they use. Do not make them meet you in English.

VARIABLES       — {PLACE}        their village or town, named.
                  {SPECIFIC_ASK} the one thing. "How the offerings at Kamrunag
                                 are actually made" is a question.
                                 "Your local knowledge" is not.
                  {CHAPTER}      the chapter it would join.

DRAFT           —
Namaste {NAME},

I write Pahari Yatri, a library of chapters about Himalayan places. We have
written about {PLACE}, and there is one thing I have not been able to get right
from outside: {SPECIFIC_ASK}.

I would like to ask you about it, and publish your answer as your own, with your
name on it, alongside our {CHAPTER} chapter.

How it would work:
- We talk, in whatever language is comfortable for you.
- I write it up and send it to you before anything is published.
- Nothing goes up until you say it is correct.
- Your name appears, or it does not — entirely your choice.
- If you change your mind at any point, even after publishing, we take it down.

There is no payment involved and nothing is being sold. We are trying to make
sure that what is written about {PLACE} is what people from {PLACE} would
actually recognise.

Please say no if you would rather not. That is a completely fine answer.

WHY THIS WORKS  — Consent terms are IN the message, not in a follow-up.
                  The withdrawal right is stated up front. The ask is one thing,
                  not "your stories".
IF NO REPLY     — One follow-up ONLY if it was sent digitally and might not have
                  been seen. Never a second ask after a non-answer in person.
RISK            — HIGHEST-RISK TEMPLATE IN THIS SYSTEM.
                  - Never publish before they confirm. The message promises this.
                  - Never call anyone a "verified local" unless a real
                    verification actually happened. It is a promise, not an
                    adjective. reputation-local-trust-agent owns this wording.
                  - Any devta, temple or ritual content from this conversation
                    goes through local-verification-editor before drafting.
                  - Do not imply this leads to business, listing, or Local
                    Connect. It does not.
```

### Template D — traveller voice request

```
CHANNEL         — Instagram DM (usually a reply to someone who commented)
RECIPIENT TYPE  — a yatri who actually walked it
GOAL            — one real first-person account
RESEARCH NEEDED — 1. Evidence they actually went. A comment describing a real
                     detail counts. A like does not.
                  2. The specific trail or place.
                  3. Something particular they said. That is the opening.

VARIABLES       — {WHAT_THEY_SAID} their own words, quoted back.
                  {PLACE}          where they went.

DRAFT           —
Hi {NAME} — you mentioned {WHAT_THEY_SAID} about {PLACE}. That is a more useful
observation than most of what gets written about it.

We publish traveller stories on Pahari Yatri — one place, one person, what
actually happened, including the parts that were not photogenic. Would you write
two or three paragraphs about it?

Your name, or anonymous, your call. You approve it before it goes up. No edits
to your meaning without asking you.

No pressure at all if you would rather not.

WHY THIS WORKS  — Quotes them, so it cannot be a template. Explicitly invites
                  the un-photogenic parts, which signals what kind of publication
                  this is.
IF NO REPLY     — None.
RISK            — If they write something that reads as an endorsement of Pahari
                  Yatri rather than an account of the place, do not publish it as
                  a story. That is a testimonial wearing a story's clothes.
```

```
{SPECIFIC_REASON_FOR_WRITING}

                  not walk through it.
                  {SPECIFIC_REASON_FOR_WRITING} has no default. If the founder
                  cannot fill it, there is no reason to send the message.
```

## 6. Tracking

Founder keeps a simple manual log. No CRM, no automation, no sequences.

| Date | Person | Channel | Ask | Response | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Health check, monthly:** if reply rate is low, the fix is **fewer and more specific messages**, never more messages. Volume is the failure mode this system exists to prevent.

## 7. Hard rules

- Never send. Ever. Drafts to the founder, founder sends personally.
- Never bulk, never sequenced, never automated.
- Never claim Local Connect exists or that anyone would be "onboarded" to it.
- Never state unverified follower counts, reach, or traction — ours or theirs.
- Never imply a partnership or endorsement that does not exist.
- Never promise payment or placement the founder has not agreed to.
- Never publish a contributor's words before they have confirmed.
- Never use "verified local" without an actual verification process behind it.
- Never use banned language.
- More than one follow-up is spam. One, with a new reason, or none.
