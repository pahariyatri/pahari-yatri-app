## 2. The five findings this month

1. **Search traffic does not touch a single named target keyword.** The real top-10 impression queries are `pahari`, `pahari path`, `mural danda trek`, `mural hills`, `pahadi log`, `jamadagni rishi temple`, `yatri way`, `patiyayatri`, `यात्री`, `himalayan folklore`. None of "Himachal travel guide", "Parvati Valley villages", "Kasol hidden places", "Kheerganga trek guide", "Kamrunag Lake story" or "Winter Himachal travel" appears. Half the real queries are brand-name confusion with the Pahari language and people.
2. **90 of 127 pages are not indexed.** This is the month's actual bottleneck. Content volume is not the constraint; discoverability is. A thin page that is not indexed and a thin page that is are commercially identical.
3. **The Instagram → chapter bridge is barely functioning.** 2 `reel_source_visit` and 7 `chapter_view` in 28 days, against 1,065 followers and thousands of weekly reach. **Tracking is confirmed live and firing** — that was unverifiable in edition #1 — so this is a real result, not a measurement gap.
4. **Every channel except Instagram is dormant, and Instagram is net-losing followers.** LinkedIn: 77 followers, zero posts ever. Facebook: 36 followers, reach of 1, live since August 2023. YouTube: 1 Short ever, against roughly 189 Instagram posts. Instagram itself: net −7 over 90 days, and its best 90-day performer was a repost of another creator, not original content.
5. **Google Business Profile is the strongest trust asset in the presence and nothing in the strategy accounts for it.** Verified, 5.0★, 29 real named reviews with owner replies dating to September 2024 — more third-party trust signal than Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube combined. It also carries the category "Tour operator", which is the most concrete instance of the brand's identity split.

## 4. What worked / what did not

**What worked.** One Reel reached 2,828 views and 2,240 reach off a single post — real distribution exists on Instagram and is not the problem. Tracking is live and firing, which means every number above is trustworthy. The website is the strongest brand asset in the presence, and the organic snippet that ranks for the brand name is on-message.

**What did not.** Publishing cadence: 2 pieces in 28 days against a 4–5/week plan. The bio link points at the homepage rather than a chapter or `/start`, so the reach that does exist has nowhere specific to go — which is exactly what 2 `reel_source_visit` events describes. Six channels sat idle. And 90 unindexed pages meant the library's depth was invisible to search regardless of what was published.

**What to repeat.** Whatever produced the 2,828-view Reel. Identify it specifically before guessing.

**What to rewrite.** The Instagram bio (three banned phrases plus a scarcity line, on the only channel that works). The bio link destination. The YouTube channel description. The Facebook page bio. The live LinkedIn job posting.

## 6. Risks and trust issues

1. **20 unlabelled stories reading as real interviewed people.** The single largest credibility exposure. The fix is free: extend the disclosure line that already works on the other 8.
2. **The five-way identity split.** Website, Instagram, Facebook, GBP, LinkedIn and YouTube each describe the brand differently, and the most-reviewed version says "tour operator." Founder decision.
3. **A live LinkedIn job posting** describing the company as curating experiences in "untouched landscapes" — a close cousin of banned language, public right now.
4. **Instagram bio** carries three banned phrases and a scarcity claim, live, on the primary growth channel.
5. **Unattributed third-party listing** (SafarCabby) presenting the brand as a Solan trekking operator, with a street address that appears nowhere else in the presence. Ownership unconfirmed.
6. **Portal trust claims.** "Verified" is used 25+ times on `app.pahariyatri.com` with no verification process found in the code, and 30 programmatic pages advertise services in states the business does not serve. Detail: `docs/growth/september-audit.md` §3.
7. **Portal measurement is dead.** 14/14 events are wired correctly and pushing into a dataLayer with no GTM container behind it, because the production Docker image was built without `NEXT_PUBLIC_GTM_ID`. Verified by `curl`, not assumed.
