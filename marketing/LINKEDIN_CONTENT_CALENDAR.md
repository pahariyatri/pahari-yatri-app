## 14. After each post — the measurement loop

- **LinkedIn native analytics** (Company Page Admin → Analytics, or your own profile's post analytics for Founder posts): impressions, reactions, comments, reshares. Check weekly, not per-post — a single post's numbers are too small to mean much yet (baseline is currently zero on everything).
- **GA4**, for any post carrying a link (only C2 this month): filter for `utm_source=linkedin`. Baseline is zero — any non-zero session after C2 posts is a genuine first.
- **Comment quality over comment count.** Per `marketing/LINKEDIN_PLAN.md` §5: a comment from a local, a guide, or a homestay owner is worth far more than ten generic "great post!" comments. Note who's engaging, not just how many.
- **Don't compare this month to any external benchmark.** There's no prior LinkedIn history for this page to compare against, and comparing 12 first-ever posts to an established account's numbers (LinkedIn's own or a marketing blog's "good engagement rate") measures the wrong thing. The comparison that matters is post 12 vs. post 1 of *this* page.
