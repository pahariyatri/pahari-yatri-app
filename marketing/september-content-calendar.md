## 7. Hard rules binding every item above

1. **No fabricated local voice.** No Reel, caption, Story or LinkedIn post may quote, paraphrase or imply a real named local person. Zero of the 28 stories in `data/stories/` carry a named consented source. Slot 5 ships in its editorial/narrated form, carrying the disclosure line verbatim, or it does not ship. An empty slot is a scheduling gap; a fabricated one is a trust incident.
2. **Every Reel points at a chapter**, never the homepage, always with its UTM campaign. The bio link is the only link that points at `/start`.
3. **Banned language, everywhere:** hidden gem · must visit · best places to visit · cheap trip · limited seats · package · explore the unexplored · untouched paradise · secret trail · book now · nestled in the lap of · breathtaking paradise · ultimate guide · unforgettable experience awaits · hidden valleys · secret trails · limited journeys. No manufactured urgency. No AI-sounding openers.
4. **Never claim Local Connect exists.** It is future intent, stated as intent.
5. **No fabricated traction.** The only real third-party trust asset is the 29 genuine 5-star Google reviews on the verified Google Business Profile. They may be cited accurately and attributed as Google reviews. Nothing else.
6. **Approval gates.** The Instagram bio replacement (Sep 23) and any edit to the live LinkedIn job posting require explicit founder approval before publishing. Everything on this calendar is a draft until the founder posts it.
